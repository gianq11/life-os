By Context
Breathing
Morning Session — 5 to 8 min. Before work starts.
Step 1 — Arrive · ~1 min
Box Breathing — 4 rounds
Inhale 4 counts. Hold 4. Exhale 4. Hold empty 4. Repeat 4x.
Step 2 — Ground · ~1 min
Body Scan
Jaw, shoulders, chest. Breathe into it. Say: "I am here. I am present. I am enough for today."
Step 3 — Connect · ~1 min
Faith Anchor
Slowly: "God goes before me today. I do not carry this alone."
Step 4 — Visualize · ~3 min
Blueprint Vision
See it clearly. Feel it in your body. Then: "This is where I am going. I have a plan. I will follow the plan."
Step 5 — Intention · 30 sec
One Word
Focus. Courage. Discipline. Trust. Breathe once. Open your eyes. Begin.
Evening Session — 5 to 7 min. After work or before bed.
Step 1 — Release · ~2 min
4-7-8 Breathing — 4 rounds
Inhale 4. Hold 7. Exhale through mouth 8.
Step 2 — Let Go · ~1 min
Release the Day
"I did what I could today. I release what I could not control."
Step 3 — Gratitude · ~1 min
3 Things
Name 3 things. Feel it.
Step 4 — Surrender · ~2 min
Rest in Faith
Hand on chest. "God, I give you today. I rest in you."
Before a sales call
Box breathing x3. "I'm prepared. I'm present. I serve this person."
When stressed
4-7-8 x3. Then: "What is the ONE thing I need to do right now?"
Before physical activity
3 sharp inhales, 1 long exhale. x3 sets.
Before sleep
4-7-8 x4-6 rounds lying down.
When procrastinating
One deep inhale, hold 2 sec, long exhale x3. Then start the smallest thing.
